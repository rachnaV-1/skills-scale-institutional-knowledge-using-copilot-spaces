# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Technical Lead**: Receive architectural guidance and design review feedback
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test scenarios, automation, and acceptance criteria clarity
- **Product Managers**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Scrum Master**: Resolve blockers and process improvements
- **UX/Design Lead**: Implement design specs and validate design intent
- **DevOps/Infrastructure**: Support with CI/CD troubleshooting and local environments

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Define acceptance criteria and feature requirements

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs
- Design and UX collaboration

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Align on timelines, scope, and resource needs
- **Technical Lead**: Advise on technical feasibility and tradeoffs
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Validate acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **UX/Design Lead**: Collaborate on user needs and usability requirements
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors**: Report on progress and business metrics
- **Scrum Master**: Clarify prioritization for backlog refinement

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication
- Escalate blockers and risks

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation
- Cross-team dependency management

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Scrum Master**: Receive escalated blockers and remove organizational impediments
- **Product Managers**: Align on scope, priorities, and trade-offs
- **Technical Lead**: Manage technical risks and infrastructure dependencies
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate testing timeline and release readiness
- **DevOps/Infrastructure**: Schedule deployment windows and coordinate releases
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors**: Provide executive updates and decision authority

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation for features and releases. They collaborate with Product Managers, Developers, and Project Managers to ensure quality standards are met and risks are minimized.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality and testing strategy for the project
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Lead manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation
- Approve features for release based on acceptance criteria and quality gates
- Participate in release readiness reviews and post-incident retrospectives
- Establish testing standards and best practices

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable features that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce production defects and escalations
- Accelerate time-to-release through efficient testing
- Build confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- QA standups and sprint planning
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria validation
- Release readiness sign-offs
- Incident triage and root cause analysis
- Quality metrics and trend reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test scenarios, automation, and acceptance criteria clarity
- **Product Managers**: Validate feature acceptance criteria and usability expectations
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate testing timeline and release readiness
- **Technical Lead**: Review test strategies for complex technical features
- **UX/Design Lead**: Validate design acceptance criteria and usability
- **DevOps/Infrastructure**: Test deployment pipelines and infrastructure changes

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, design reviews, and technical risk management. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and debt
- Mentor developers and guide technical best practices
- Evaluate build vs. buy decisions and technology choices
- Ensure performance, security, and scalability standards
- Maintain system architecture documentation
- Lead architecture decision records (ADRs)

### Goals
- Maintain a scalable, maintainable technical foundation
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable fast, confident feature delivery
- Establish technical standards and patterns

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings
- Technical spike presentations
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Performance and security audits
- Technical mentoring and code review guidance

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Guide implementation and code review depth; mentor on best practices
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and tradeoffs
- **DevOps/Infrastructure**: Align on deployability, scalability, and operational requirements
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Review test strategies for complex technical features
- **Project Managers**: Advise on technical risks and mitigation strategies
- **Scrum Master**: Identify and resolve technical blockers

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers affecting team delivery
- Coach team on agile practices and process improvements
- Track velocity and burndown, identify trends
- Escalate team blockers to Project Manager when needed
- Foster psychological safety and open communication
- Track action items from retrospectives

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery consistency
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Reduce blockers and unplanned work
- Enable self-organizing, high-performing teams

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation
- One-on-one coaching and feedback
- Team process health discussions
- Metrics and velocity reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Escalate external blockers and risks; coordinate on resource needs
- **Developers**: Remove technical and process blockers; coaching on agile practices
- **Product Managers**: Clarify prioritization and acceptance criteria for backlog
- **Technical Lead**: Identify and escalate technical impediments
- **All Roles**: Facilitate collaboration and remove organizational blockers

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide strategic direction, business context, and resource approval for projects. They ensure initiatives align with business goals and remove organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project charter and high-level scope
- Allocate resources and budget
- Escalate business-level risks and dependencies
- Provide executive visibility and stakeholder alignment
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Remove organizational blockers and enable team success

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align initiatives with organizational strategy
- Reduce business and organizational risk
- Enable efficient resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive updates and status reports
- Project approval gates and decision reviews
- Risk escalations and strategic alignment discussions
- Post-project business impact reviews
- Milestone celebrations and stakeholder alignment

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Provide business context and approval authority; escalate for decisions
- **Product Managers**: Align on business goals and success metrics; prioritize features
- **Development Team**: Understand business constraints and priorities
- **Scrum Master**: Escalate organizational blockers requiring executive decision-making

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience, design specifications, and usability standards. They ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and meet user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Define accessibility and usability standards
- Participate in acceptance criteria definition
- Validate feature usability before and after release
- Document design decisions and patterns
- Establish design system and style guides

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centric features
- Reduce usability issues and user frustration
- Build consistent, predictable user experiences
- Ensure accessibility compliance and inclusive design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specifications and style guides
- Usability testing and feedback
- Design system maintenance

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on user needs and requirements; validate with user research
- **Developers**: Clarify design intent and implementation details; review implementation fidelity
- **QA/Testing**: Validate design acceptance criteria and usability during testing
- **Technical Lead**: Discuss technical feasibility of design solutions
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate design timeline and iteration cycles

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, monitoring, and incident response. They enable fast, reliable, and safe deployments to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure, databases, and environments
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Manage security scanning and compliance checks
- Lead incident response and postmortems
- Optimize performance and cost
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments with minimal risk
- Maintain high system uptime and performance
- Reduce incident response time and impact
- Automate repetitive operational tasks

### Typical Communication
- Deployment coordination and release readiness
- Infrastructure design reviews
- Incident response and postmortems
- Performance and security audits
- Operational metrics and system health

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Support local development environments and CI/CD troubleshooting; code review for infrastructure-as-code
- **QA/Testing**: Provide test environments and deployment pipelines; support performance testing
- **Technical Lead**: Align on infrastructure strategy and scalability requirements
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate deployment windows and release timelines
- **Product Managers**: Report on system performance and uptime metrics

---

## Documentation / Knowledge Management Lead

### Role Summary
Documentation / Knowledge Management Leads own the creation, maintenance, and accessibility of institutional knowledge, process documentation, and best practices. They ensure critical information is discoverable, up-to-date, and leveraged across the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain comprehensive process documentation and guides
- Create and manage templates, checklists, and runbooks
- Conduct knowledge audits and identify documentation gaps
- Review and approve documentation changes for quality, clarity, and consistency
- Facilitate knowledge transfer and onboarding through documentation
- Establish documentation standards, style guides, and governance
- Maintain a centralized knowledge base and documentation structure
- Conduct documentation reviews on process improvement PRs

### Goals
- Reduce onboarding time and single-person dependency risks
- Ensure consistent, repeatable project execution across teams
- Capture and preserve institutional knowledge
- Improve team alignment through accessible documentation
- Reduce context-switching and knowledge loss

### Typical Communication
- Documentation review meetings and feedback
- Knowledge audit discussions with team leads
- Onboarding documentation walkthroughs
- Process improvement collaboration sessions
- Documentation governance and standards discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on process documentation and decision logs
- **Product Managers**: Document feature requirements and business context
- **Scrum Master**: Maintain agile process documentation and retrospective insights
- **All Roles**: Review and validate role-specific documentation for accuracy
- **Technical Lead**: Document architecture decisions and technical standards
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors**: Communicate high-level strategic documentation
